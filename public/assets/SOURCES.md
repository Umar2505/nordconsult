# Passport artwork source

The cover emblem uses [Emblem of Uzbekistan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Emblem_of_Uzbekistan.svg), an official symbol listed as public domain on Wikimedia Commons. The site renders this SVG as a gold line texture on the 3D cover and as a faint interior watermark.

Cover layout and lettering were matched against [a photograph of the 2019 international passport](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Front_cover_of_Uzbekistan_international_passport_(started_issuing_January_1,_2019).jpg). The photograph itself is not bundled with the site.

Capital-city photography is loaded from Unsplash and credited in the interface: Tashkent by ZBS, Helsinki by Tapio Haaja, Stockholm by Raphael Andres, Oslo by Christoffer Engström, Berlin by Florian Wehde, Rome by David Köhler, Amsterdam via Unsplash, and Beijing by Ling Tang.

The interior story text and ornamental paper design are original editorial artwork for Nord Consult; they do not represent an official identity or visa page.
