N ✦PREPARING YOUR JOURNEY
This fictional journey follows Alex Karimov from Uzbekistan, studying Computer Science. The example profile has a budget of €10,000 per year, IELTS 6.5 and a scholarship priority. The journey explores destinations, selects Finland, prepares documents and an application, receives a fictional admission, then proceeds to visa preparation and departure. Scene five presents independently published exchange stories that are not Nord Consult client outcomes. Scene seven uses credited prototype photography to present Trondheim and NTNU as an illustrative study destination. Scene eight uses fictional documents, licensed development photography clearly labeled as placeholders, to illustrate the preparation process. These examples do not represent verified client outcomes, company staff, a university partnership, or guaranteed admission.
